# Privacy Policy — Tintuc

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Tintuc ("the App") is an application designed to automate news content processing and publishing videos to Facebook Pages using the Meta Graph API.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how that information is used, and how users can request its deletion.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the App may access:

- Facebook Page identifiers and Page names.
- Page access tokens required to perform authorized publishing operations.
- Video files, captions, titles, and other content provided for publishing.
- Basic technical information necessary to operate, troubleshoot, and maintain the App.

The App only accesses information necessary for its intended functionality and the permissions granted by the user.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the App is used to:

- Publish authorized video content to the selected Facebook Page.
- Retrieve publishing results and video status.
- Diagnose publishing errors and improve application reliability.
- Maintain the security and proper operation of the App.

The App does not use Facebook information for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and other sensitive configuration values should be stored securely and must not be exposed in source code, public repositories, or application logs.

The App does not intentionally retain personal information beyond what is necessary for its intended operation.

Where information is stored, reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect it against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

## 5. Data Sharing

The App does not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to Meta Platforms, Inc. as necessary to authenticate requests and publish content through the Meta Graph API. Data may also be processed by infrastructure or service providers explicitly used by the App.

The App does not share information with unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality or meet applicable legal obligations.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with Meta Platforms, Inc. and other services required for news processing, media generation, or publishing.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The App is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. If personal information is found to have been collected improperly, users may contact us to request its review and deletion.
